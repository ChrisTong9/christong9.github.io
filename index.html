About

01

Hi, I'm Zhongyi (Chris) Tong, a B.S. student in Computer Science at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI).

Aug 2024 – May 2027 (expected) · GPA 3.78 / 4.0

My research interests center on multimodal learning and generation, as well as AI agents. I'm interested in how models understand and generate content across modalities, and how agents use reasoning and tools to carry out complex tasks.

I'm happy to be working with Prof. Yapeng Tian as a research assistant in the Computer Vision and Multimodal Computing (CVMC) Lab at The University of Texas at Dallas. My work there focuses on audio understanding and editing.

At WPI, I also work with Prof. Kyumin Lee in the Info Lab, and with Prof. Benjamin Nephew and Prof. Dmitry Korkin in King's Lab.

Alongside research, I'm an artist with a practice that includes installation work. I enjoy playing basketball, listening to music, watching films, and visiting art museums.

  2. 2026

    A Two-Stage Fusion Framework for Few-Shot Species Identification from Melting Curve Data

    Chengqian Zhang, Kyumin Lee, and Zhongyi Tong

    ACM BCB 2026

    A two-stage framework fusing BERT-encoded statistical prompts with time-series patch embeddings to identify species from DNA melting curves.

    93.08% mean accuracy across 65 shark and ray species, with only three training samples per species.
